# The Gaia Vision

We envision a world where every open-source project can achieve true autonomy—becoming unstoppable digital organisms that thrive long after their creators have moved on.

## The Core Promise

Gaia exists to solve the fundamental paradox of open-source: projects need centralized agility to begin, but require decentralized resilience to endure. We believe the answer lies not in choosing one over the other, but in creating a path from one to the other.

Our mission is to give projects life—to transform them from fragile codebases dependent on human maintainers into self-sustaining ecosystems that evolve on their own terms.

## What "Unstoppable" Means

An unstoppable project is one that:
- **Sustains itself** through built-in economic mechanisms
- **Governs itself** through community-driven decision-making
- **Evolves itself** through continuous, autonomous development
- **Survives its creators** by becoming truly decentralized

## The Long View

We're not just building another code hosting platform. We're creating the infrastructure for digital life—the soil where projects can grow from human creations into autonomous beings.

This vision guides every decision we make, from the technical architecture to the economic models we explore. It's why we'll eventually migrate Gaia itself to run on Gaia—to prove that what we build can sustain even the most complex projects.
